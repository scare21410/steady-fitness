# Steady Fitness direction

## Available now

- JSON-configured guided kettlebell workouts, warm-up and cool-down.
- One-minute strength slots with low starting rep targets and selectable progression.
- Matching illustrations, full-workout progress, interval timers, audio and pause/skip.
- Responsive TV, desktop and mobile layout, with remote and keyboard controls.
- Browser-local activity records; existing kettlebell history remains readable.

## Next, when requested

1. Activity history screen: date, type, duration, completion and notes.
2. Gym visits: manually log a visit, exercises, sets, reps and weights.
3. Shared progress view: weekly consistency and trends across home and gym training.
4. Export/import and optional cross-device storage. Design backup and privacy before adding synchronization.

Keep the tracker centered on showing up and gradual progress. Keep playback separate from tracking so a gym visit does not need to run a timer.
